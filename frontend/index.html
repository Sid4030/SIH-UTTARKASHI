STAGE 1

🌐 Real-Time Telemetry Feeds

Every 60 seconds, BhuRakshak polls live zero-authentication endpoints for Uttarkashi district (30.73° N, 78.45° E):

  • Open-Meteo & IMD Radar: Hourly rainfall intensity (mm/hr), 24h antecedent accumulation, and 7-day saturation index.
  • USGS Real-Time Earthquake Stream: Regional seismic events (M > 2.5) deriving peak horizontal ground acceleration ($k_h$).
  • Google Earth Engine / Copernicus: Sentinel-2 10m multispectral imagery (NDVI for slope scarification, NDWI for river swell).
STAGE 2

⚖️ Dynamic Geotechnical Physics

Raw rainfall is immediately coupled into the Mohr-Coulomb Limit Equilibrium equation to compute the dynamic Factor of Safety (FS):

$$FS = \frac{c' + (\gamma z \cos^2\beta - u - \gamma z k_h \sin\beta\cos\beta)\tan\phi'}{\gamma z \sin\beta\cos\beta + \gamma z k_h \cos^2\beta}$$

Where pore-water pressure $u(t) = \frac{I}{100} \cdot 12 + \frac{A_{24}}{150} \cdot 10\text{ kPa}$. If $FS < 1.0$, shear failure is mathematically inevitable.

STAGE 3

🗺️ Dynamic Zonation & Expansion

Hazard perimeters are not static lines — they expand and contract dynamically based on instantaneous moisture:

  • Red Zone Trigger: Settlements where $FS < 1.0$ or rainfall exceeds the Himalayan threshold ($I = 14.82 \cdot D^{-0.39}$) turn Red instantly.
  • Debris Flow Tracking: HydroSHEDS 30m flow direction grids trace runout channels down to the Bhagirathi riverbed.
  • Highway Hazard: NH-108 segments within 250m of active scarps are flagged for immediate transit halt.
STAGE 4

🛡️ Dynamic Relocation Reallocation

When habitations enter the Red Zone, the THRIVE engine calculates relocation logistics:

  • Safe Haven Matching: Matches endangered villages to safe sites with $FS > 1.8$, slope $< 12^\circ$, and zero flood exposure.
  • Carrying Capacity Ledger: Enforces NDMA standard $45\text{ m}^2/\text{person}$ and $70\text{ lpcd}$ water, deducting headroom in real time.
  • Evacuation Corridor: Dijkstra terrain routing computes optimal road transit times and bypasses blocked highway sectors.
